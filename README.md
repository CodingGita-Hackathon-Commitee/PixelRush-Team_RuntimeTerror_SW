# 🍱 TiffinBox: Meal Subscription App

## Team: RuntimeTerror

### Event/Track: PixelRush Software

## Problem Statement: #10

## 🚨 The Problem

### Students and young professionals living away from home heavily rely on daily tiffin (meal) services. However, the current ecosystem for managing these subscriptions is highly disorganized, manual, and frustrating for consumers:

#### Fragmented Communication: Daily menus are sent sporadically through unorganized WhatsApp messages.

#### Cumbersome Modifications: Simply skipping or pausing a meal requires making a direct phone call to the vendor.

#### Manual Tracking: Payments, dues, and meal counts are tracked on paper, which is prone to errors and loss.

#### Lack of Transparency: Users frequently struggle to know exactly how many meals they have left in their current plan or how much money is due.

## 💡 The Challenge

### Design a Meal Subscription experience that makes choosing a plan, managing daily meals, and tracking payments simple and clear.

## 🚀 Our Solution: TiffinBox

### TiffinBox is a centralized meal subscription app designed to bridge the gap between tiffin service providers and students. It eliminates the hassle of manual tracking and disjointed communication by offering a seamless, intuitive digital experience.

### By digitizing the entire process, TiffinBox brings transparency, ease of use, and automation to daily meal management.

## ✨ Key Features to be Implemented

#### Plan Selection: Easily browse, compare, and choose meal subscription plans that fit your specific schedule and budget.

#### Digital Menu Management: View daily and weekly menus directly within the app—no more searching through WhatsApp chat histories.

#### One-Tap Meal Management: Skip or pause an upcoming meal with a single tap. The vendor is automatically notified, and your meal count adjusts dynamically.

#### Transparent Dashboard: Instantly view your remaining meals, current dues, and track your overall usage analytics.

#### Integrated Payment Tracking: Streamlined digital payment tracking to eliminate paper records and billing disputes.
