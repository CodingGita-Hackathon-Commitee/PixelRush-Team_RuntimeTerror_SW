## Problem Statement-10 : TIFFINBOX, MEAL SUBSCRIPTION APP

### Students living away from home depend on tiffin services for daily meals. But managing them is a hassle. Menus come through WhatsApp messages, skipping a meal means calling the vendor, payments are tracked on paper, and it’s hard to know how many meals are left or how much is due.
### Your challenge is to design a Meal Subscription experience that makes choosing a plan, managing daily meals, and tracking payments simple and clear.
